03 / 04 BEHIND THE CAMERA

A little about me.

Hi, I’m Mara. I’m a photographer drawn to the in-between.

The breath before a laugh. The walk home after the party. The way a landscape holds the marks of the people who belong to it. I’ve spent the last eight years making pictures that find the extraordinary in ordinary life.

Moving between portraits and long-form documentary work, I try to meet every person and place with patience, curiosity, and respect. Based in Brooklyn and always happy to pack light.
